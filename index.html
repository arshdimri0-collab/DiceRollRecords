[ Press Release ] OCT 04, 2026

Dice Roll Records now live to serve new artists

Official announcement marking the formal launch of Dice Roll Records as an independent artist platform.
Open Bulletin ↓

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

Dice Roll Records today officially announced its launch, opening its doors as an independent, artist-first record label designed to discover, nurture, and elevate emerging musical talent. Operating under a modern, artist-centric philosophy, Dice Roll Records arrives with a clear mission: to give high-potential musicians the resources, creative control, and global platform needed to build sustainable long-term careers.

The label’s core framework provides comprehensive artist development, including tailored A&R guidance, digital strategy, royalty transparency, and streaming optimization while maintaining complete creative control for creators.

[ Founder Letter ] OCT 04, 2026

Its now all tangible

Arsh our flagship artist and founder reveals his vision with this label

Hi!!!!!!!!!!!
Arsh here, I'm so glad you all came here. Its been a month I've been preparing for the launch of this website and my independent record label.

This record label is a vision of mine where us artist gets to explore ourselves through music, because every vision deserves a stage. Every release of mine was, is,will be released through this label.

At the moment we don’t have much resources to sign other artists but it’s a dream goal that somewhere in future we get to build a system where we would get to help other artists and musicians too.

Its all pretty surreal that a simple idea has now been slowly shaping into a tangible reality.

Many things are to come on this website so keep checking, all my catalogue and deep dives and many more to come……………
