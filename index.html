[ Behind The Tape ] OCT 19, 2026

VERY SPONTANEOUS

Arsh, shares BTS of making of their first mixtape and reveals future plans

All I wanna say is that, IMPROPER CODINGS was not something that was meant to be. It had no head,no tail, popped out of a random instagram post. So I created a new id of mine to focus on music projects called "twinkonaguitar". I just randomly posted a pic with that windows player UI snapchat filter captioned, "improper codings coming soon". Nothing like that ever existed in reality.

I was wondering what to do now? I had nothing that resonates with this vision, no nothing. As far as I remember next day I just randomly start throwing out random song titles to make a tracklist. I got to my regular place of recording next morning, picked up my guitar, strummed some chords tryna come up with a melody. It was nothing different, I'm pretty used to doing this stuff but the actual difference is that, this time all of these tracks are going to end up on streaming. A fairly new experience.

Recordings started on end of March, the original idea was to record and put each track on Soundcloud first before officially releasing on all streaming services but as we all know that idea was shelfed because it was boring. The original mixtape was planned to be 16 tracks long but I didn’t like working that long it was getting hell boring and I needed to get it done.

The main tracks were all recorded in a single track with instruments and vocals being recorded simultaneously. They were only supposed to be demos but I kinda liked the grit these recordings had in them so I kept them as it is, because I fear the rawnesss and emotions in these demos cannot be replicated and I loved it . The whole mixtape is in the order of the first recorded tracks being first and the last being the last. So the first track to be ever recorded was Wise Words(Intro), it was recorded way back in 2024, but I liked the essence of it so I decided to break off this mixtape with this track. The outro was recorded somewhat at the end but the last track to be recorded was Chasin' Sunset.

I really liked this mixtape it has a lot of feelings in it and something to say. As minimal it sounds, as big image it portrays to the audience through simplicity and bad quality. I would keep this style for a while and future releases and would experiment more. This year was crazy but not still crazy enough because we still have to take one last bite of the apple. This year might be getting nearer to a closure but my musical journey has just begin and there is hell more to come next year. I just cant wait to unravel my bag and spread my art in the world, its very very beautiful and whimsical.

LOVE YALL<3
XX

[ Founder Letter ] OCT 04, 2026

Its now all tangible

Arsh our flagship artist and founder reveals his vision with this label

Hi!!!!!!!!!!!
Arsh here, I'm so glad you all came here. Its been a month I've been preparing for the launch of this website and my independent record label.

This record label is a vision of mine where us artist gets to explore ourselves through music, because every vision deserves a stage. Every release of mine was, is,will be released through this label.

At the moment we don’t have much resources to sign other artists but it’s a dream goal that somewhere in future we get to build a system where we would get to help other artists and musicians too.

Its all pretty surreal that a simple idea has now been slowly shaping into a tangible reality.

Many things are to come on this website so keep checking, all my catalogue and deep dives and many more to come……………

LOVE YALL<3
