[ Music Review ] SEPT 2026

Single Review: Arsh Dimri — "Boys Like You"

Boys Like You stands out as a compelling statement from Arsh Dimri. Blending raw, introspective lyricism with precise production, the track captures a distinct mood of self-reflection and retro warmth.

The instrumentation leans heavily into classic dynamic contrasts—letting atmospheric textures breathe before introducing driven drum patterns. Dimri’s vocal performance maintains an authentic clarity throughout, anchoring the record's emotional weight.

As catalog release #001 for Dice Roll Records, it sets an ambitious standard for what the label aims to represent in independent music.
